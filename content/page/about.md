### About WJH.now

**What Just Happened NOW**  
*The Latest From Science, Tech and The World Around Us*

Look, the internet is loud.  
Every day there's a new gadget, a new space photo, a new "scientists say".  
Most of it is buried in 2000-word articles you’ll never finish.

That’s why we made **WJH.now**.

We read the papers, the leaks, and the weird research so you don’t have to.  
Then we just tell you what went down and why it matters — usually in the time it takes to finish a coffee.

### What do we actually write about?

Honestly? Three things:

1. **Science** – Space, biology, physics. The "wait, boiling in space is backwards?" kind of facts.
2. **Tech** – AI, phones, leaks. What’s coming, what’s real, and what it means for you.
3. **Everything else** – The random discoveries and trends that are quietly changing the world.

We’re based in India. Started in 2026. Just a small team obsessed with “huh, that’s cool”.

We’re not a traditional news site.  
We’re not trying to be first.  
We’re trying to be the site that finally explains it without making your brain hurt.

Got a weird science fact or a tip?  
Email us at **info@wjhnow.com** — we read everything.

### Who Writes This

**Mr. JH**  
I’m the science nerd. I dig through research papers and turn “quantum entanglement” into “here’s why it’s weird”. If it involves space or a “did you know”, I probably wrote it.

**Mr. Wnow**  
I’m the tech guy. Leaks, gadgets, AI, and all the “how does this actually work” stuff. I test the claims so you get the truth without the marketing fluff.
