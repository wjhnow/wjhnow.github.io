# Redmi 17 5G: Xiaomi Chose Battery Over Everything Else

Xiaomi launched the Redmi 17 5G in India on September 3 and I’m still turning it over in my head.

The battery is the whole story. India gets 7,900mAh while the global version sits at 7,500mAh. It charges at 45W and they actually included the charger. Reverse charging is there too. If your current phone is dead by dinner this one can stretch to three days without trying hard.

Price after bank offers comes to ₹23,999 for 6GB + 128GB and ₹26,999 for the 8GB model. Sale starts September 10 on Amazon and Mi stores. Colours are black, blue and an orange vegan leather one.

The screen is where it gets weird.

Big 6.9-inch 120Hz panel, Gorilla Glass 7i, decent brightness. And then you notice it’s HD+. In 2026. At this price most phones have already moved to FHD+ AMOLED. Xiaomi just decided the battery was more important. You feel it the moment you start watching anything longer than a short video.

Chip is the new Snapdragon 4 Gen 5, first time in India. It’s an efficiency part. Normal apps are fine. Games are not. Same compromise as the display — everything else got cut so the battery could stay huge.

Camera is a 50MP main sensor. Daylight is okay, night is average. At least the software is current: HyperOS 3 on Android 16 from day one. Headphone jack is still here, stereo speakers, IP64, microSD slot. The usual checklist.

I keep coming back to the same thought. This phone does one job better than anything else at the price and almost everything else worse. If battery life is the only thing that actually ruins your day, nothing around ₹24,000 comes close.  For the rest of us the compromises pile up fast once you actually start using it day to day.
