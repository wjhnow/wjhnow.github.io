Redmi 17 5G launched and I need to talk about it because the choices Xiaomi made are confusing.



## The Good Part



**7900mAh battery**. 

With **45W charging** and they are actually putting the **charger in box**. Not a 10W brick. **22.5W reverse charging** too. 

In India we got **400mah more than global** which is 7500mah. Small win but still. 

If you’re a delivery guy, or travel a lot, or just hate charging, this is the phone. **It’ll do 3 days easy.**



## Price & Sale



**Price is 23,999 for 6GB 128GB** and **26,999 for 8GB 128GB** after bank offers. **MRP is 39,999 and 41,999** which is a joke. 

**Sale starts September 10** on **Amazon and Mi stores**. 



## Now the part that annoyed me



**Display. 6.9 inch. 120Hz. Gorilla Glass 7i. 825 nits peak brightness.** 

All sounds fine until you see **HD+**. Not FHD+. **HD+**. 

In 2026. At 24k. 

I understand the logic. **7900mah battery + HD+ panel = insane battery life.** 

But man, every other phone at this price gives you **FHD+ AMOLED**. iQOO, Realme, even some Motorolas. 

Watching videos on HD+ feels like a downgrade in 2026. Xiaomi chose battery over everything and it shows.



## Processor & Performance



**Processor is Snapdragon 4 Gen 5 on 4nm**. First phone in India with it. 

It’s an **efficiency chip. Not for gaming.** 

Daily stuff will be smooth. Instagram, YouTube, WhatsApp, no issues. 

But don’t buy this expecting **BGMI 60fps or Genshin**. You’ll be disappointed. 

That’s the trade off for 3 day battery.



## Camera & Other Stuff



**Camera is 50MP main sensor**. It’s Redmi so expectations should be realistic. 

Daylight photos are fine. Night is average with noise. 



**Software side is actually good. HyperOS 3 on Android 16** out of the box. 

That means **3 years of OS updates and 4 years security**. Better than most at this price. 

They also kept the **headphone jack**. **Stereo speakers** are there. **IP64 rating** for splash resistance. 

**Bluetooth 5.1 and NFC** included too. 

Build wise it’s plastic but with **Gorilla Glass 7i** on front.



## So who is this for?



Honestly? **People whose biggest complaint is "my phone dies".** 

If you charge twice a day and want that to stop, **nothing at 24k beats this.**



## Who should skip?



Anyone who cares about **screen quality, gaming, or camera.** 

At 24k you can get better **AMOLED displays and faster chips**. You just won’t get 7900mah.



**Xiaomi basically made a power bank with a phone attached.** 

At 19k this would be the default recommendation. At 24k it’s a niche pick.



I’m still torn. Battery life is amazing but the HD+ hurts.
