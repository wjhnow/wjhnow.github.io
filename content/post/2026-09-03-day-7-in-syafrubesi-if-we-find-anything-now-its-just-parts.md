## Day 7 in Syafrubesi: 'If We Find Anything Now, It's Just Parts

**SYAFRUBESI, Nepal** — The smell of wet river mud hits you long before you reach the banks of the Trishuli.

It’s day seven, and the search teams here have mostly put down their shovels. Nobody is looking for survivors under the silt anymore.

"If we find anything now, it’s just parts," said Pemba Sherpa, a local volunteer whose brother’s shop was swept away when the river rose. He was leaning against a stalled army truck, his boots caked in grey crust. "The water came down like cement."

What happened on August 26 wasn't just rain. High up on Langtang Lirung, roughly 5,200 meters up, a massive shelf of ice and rock broke off and plummeted straight into the narrow Lende Khola. That sent a wall of mud and mountain debris roaring down toward Rasuwa and Nuwakot.

Right now, army diggers are trying to clear what's left of the Pasang Lhamu Highway near Syafrubesi. Large chunks of the road — Kathmandu’s primary route to the border with China — simply collapsed into the river. Up in the higher pockets of the valley, roads are completely non-existent.

A district administrative officer in Syafrubesi, who asked not to use his name because he wasn't cleared to speak with reporters, shook his head when asked about timeline estimates. "Forget clearing the roads," he said, waving toward the ridge. "In some places up there, there’s no ground left to build a road on. We're dropping food out of helicopters when the fog clears, but it's not enough."

Meanwhile, the numbers keep climbing. Government updates now put the death toll across central Nepal and nearby sectors of Tibet at over 1,200, with another 3,900 people officially listed as missing. Down in Nuwakot, displaced families are sleeping on school floors, and cell service is still out across most of the upper VDCs.

And the weather isn't helping. Rain started falling again over the ridges this morning. Army officers along the river say they're watching the upper Trishuli closely — if those temporary mud dams higher up collapse, another wave of silt is going to wash right through what's left of the search area.
