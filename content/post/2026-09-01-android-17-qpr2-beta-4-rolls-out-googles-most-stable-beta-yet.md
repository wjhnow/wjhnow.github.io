# Android 17 QPR2 Beta 4: Pixel 11 Support Arrives And Notification Intelligence Is Cooked 💀

Android 17 QPR2 Beta 4 dropped over the weekend. Google also finally let Pixel 11 owners into the beta program yesterday, which is late but whatever.

Usually these late-cycle QPR builds are battery disasters. This one somehow made it to "Platform Stability" without turning the phone into a hand warmer. Miracle.

The first thing that hits after flashing is Notification Intelligence. Google just slapped "AI" on the old notification history and called it a dashboard. In theory it sorts your life. In practice it marks active family WhatsApp groups as "low priority spam" and pushes some random clothing brand promo to the top of the lock screen. To fix it you have to crawl through three sub-menus. Miss a toggle and you miss actual messages. Dumb.

There’s also a new Status Bar menu hiding in Settings > System. You can kill the mute and vibrate icons finally. They’ve been hogging space next to the battery for years.

Lock screen overlays are back too. Calendar and weather widgets that sit on the glass. Looked clean for five minutes until a notification rolled in and the text crashed right into the Spotify player. Classic.

## The community vibe right now

Scrolling Reddit and X is weird. No one’s posting bricked device threads. People are just complaining less.

The standby drain from Beta 3 is gone. Phones make it to dinner now instead of dying at 3 PM. Animations are smoother. Bluetooth isn’t disconnecting in cars every 10 minutes. Instagram camera stopped crashing instantly.

That’s not normal for a QPR2 beta.

## So should you install it?

For Pixel 11 and Fold owners this is the first beta available. Google locked the APIs so it won’t nuke apps.

Most people are still on Android 14 anyway. Update if you want, just don’t expect the new AI to know the difference between your mom and a Shein ad.
