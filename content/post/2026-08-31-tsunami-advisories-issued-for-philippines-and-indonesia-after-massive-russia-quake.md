Tsunami advisories issued for Philippines and Indonesia after massive Russia quake

Coastal communities across the Philippines and Indonesia were put on alert today after a massive earthquake off Russia triggered tsunami advisories across the Pacific.

What we know so far

**Philippines**

The Philippine volcanology agency warned waves under 1 metre could reach coastal areas by early afternoon. Residents in over 20 provinces were told to avoid beaches and low-lying coastal zones.

**Indonesia**

Indonesia’s meteorology and geophysics agency issued early warnings for parts of North Sulawesi, North Maluku, West Papua, and Gorontalo. While no evacuation orders have been announced, residents are urged to stay away from the shoreline and remain alert.

**Japan on alert**

In Japan, a 30cm wave reached Hokkaido, triggering tsunami alerts and evacuation orders across eastern and northern coastlines. The Japan Meteorological Agency has upgraded its warning, predicting waves up to 3 metres from Hokkaido to Wakayama.

As a precaution, workers at the Fukushima nuclear plant were evacuated and train services along parts of Japan’s eastern coast were suspended.

**Pacific-wide impact**

USGS and US Tsunami Warning Centers issued alerts for hazardous waves across the Pacific basin. Governments urged people in coastal regions to evacuate to higher ground and stay away from beaches until further notice.

Emergency agencies continue to monitor the situation closely.
